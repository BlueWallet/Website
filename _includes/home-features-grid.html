What you can do with BlueWallet

Besides receiving, sending and storing Bitcoin, you can do a lot more.

Self-custody — Your keys, your bitcoin

Don't leave your coins with others. Own your Bitcoin. Your keys never leave your device.

Multisig Vault Wallets

The best security available on the Bitcoin protocol. Wallets with keys for multiple devices.

Fees & Transactions control

Control fees. Easily Speed up, Cancel or Batch transactions. With RBF and CPFP.

Hardware wallets support

Connect Coldcard, Passport, Jade, Keystone and other PSBT-compatible hardware wallets while keeping keys offline.

Notifications

Never miss a transaction. Push notifications for pending and confirmed transactions.

Plausible Deniability

Create multiple storages or fake ones in case of a forced disclosure.

Open source & privacy-first

Don't trust, verify! New addresses automatically, Coin Control, Payjoin, Paycodes, connect to your own node.

Import & Recover wallets

A "magical form" where you can try to import whatever you got. With search and discover capabilities.
