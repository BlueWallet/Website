Wallets supported

BlueWallet supports a wide range of Bitcoin wallet formats, making it easier to recover old wallets and maintain compatibility with other services.

Bitcoin Wallet

Recommended

The best choice for most people.

Create a modern Native SegWit wallet with multiple addresses, lower transaction fees and full control over your bitcoin.

Watch-only Wallet

Monitor your bitcoin without storing private keys on your phone.

Track balances, generate receiving addresses and review transactions while keeping your signing keys safely offline.

Multisig Vault

Protect your bitcoin with multiple keys.

A multisig vault requires more than one key to approve a transaction, reducing the risk of losing funds if a single device or backup is compromised.

Ideal for larger balances, shared custody and long-term savings.

Lightning Wallet

Send and receive instant Bitcoin payments over the Lightning Network.

Connect BlueWallet to your own Lightning node and manage fast, low-cost payments from the same app.

Wrapped SegWit

Advanced Mode

A SegWit wallet designed for compatibility with older exchanges and services.

Single Address

Advanced Mode

A wallet that reuses the same receiving address.

Useful for specific workflows, but not recommended for everyday use because address reuse reduces privacy.

Legacy

Import older Bitcoin wallets with addresses beginning with 1.

Legacy wallets remain supported, although newer address formats generally provide better efficiency and lower fees.

Electrum

Import wallets created with Electrum-specific seed formats.

BlueWallet automatically recognizes supported Electrum seeds and restores the appropriate wallet.

BRD and Breadwallet

Recover wallets created with BRD or Breadwallet.

BlueWallet supports their custom recovery format, helping you regain access without depending on the original app.

Create a new Bitcoin wallet

Getting started takes only a few seconds.

Choose Bitcoin when creating a wallet and BlueWallet will generate a modern, multi-address Native SegWit wallet—the recommended option for most users.

Your recovery phrase is created on your device. Write it down, store it somewhere safe and never share it with anyone.

Bring your existing wallet

Already have a Bitcoin wallet? Bring it with you.

Paste or scan your recovery phrase, private key, public key or wallet backup. BlueWallet will identify the format and attempt to restore the correct wallet automatically.

BlueWallet supports common Bitcoin standards as well as several wallet-specific recovery formats.
