BlueWallet Portfolio

Built for Bitcoin users. Stack sats and watch your portfolio grow. See live Bitcoin price moves, mempool state with next blocks, and current fee estimates.

Watch-only. Live price. Mempool view.

Watch your Bitcoin safely

Everything is watch-only. You can see balances and history-related metrics, but you cannot send, spend, or import private keys.

Addresses or xpubs

Add a Bitcoin address or an xpub / ypub / zpub to track funds as they move in.

Optional labels

Give each account a name, for example “Cold storage”, so the list stays readable.

Watch-only by design

See balances and history-related metrics. There is no way to send or import keys.

Remove anytime

Drop a watched account from your list whenever you no longer need it.

Portfolio at a glance

See your total value in USD, with your BTC balance right underneath.
Browse how holdings changed over 1 week, 1 month, 6 months, 1 year, or all.
Review unrealized return, average buy price, and cost basis, based on when your confirmed coins first appeared.

Mempool and fees

See mempool depth and the latest blocks as they come in.
Check estimated fees for the next block, ~30 minutes, or ~1 hour.
Follow the live BTC price in USD, with a chart for 1 hour, 6 hours, or 1 day.

Understand your coins

Open a UTXO breakdown of confirmed coins.
For each coin, see amount, current USD value, and cost basis.
Tap a coin for more detail, including when it showed up and the historical price used for cost basis.

Fits how you browse

Keep it beside the page you’re on, or open it full-width when you want more room.

Side panel

Opens from the toolbar so it stays beside whatever you’re already looking at.

Full-page view

More room when you want it. Price and portfolio sit side by side on wider screens.

Light or dark

Follows your system appearance automatically.

Remembers the tab

Comes back to Price or Portfolio, whichever you looked at last.

What it won’t do

By design. If you need to send, recover a wallet, or hold keys, use the BlueWallet app.

No sending or seeds

No sending, Lightning, or wallet recovery from seed phrases. Private keys never belong here.

USD only

Values are shown in US dollars. That’s the unit for price, portfolio, and cost basis.

Confirmed coins

Cost basis and the UTXO list track confirmed coins. Unconfirmed activity isn’t treated the same way.
