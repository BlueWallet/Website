---
title: Which wallet type should I create?
categories: [bitcoin-wallet]
---

For most people, the default **Bitcoin wallet** (Native SegWit, multiple addresses). Use [Watch-only](/watch-only/) to monitor keys you keep offline, a [Multisig Vault](/multisig-wallet/) for larger savings, and [Lightning](/lightning/) for small, instant payments from your own node. Wrapped SegWit, Single Address, and Legacy are under Advanced Mode for compatibility or recovery, not everyday use.
