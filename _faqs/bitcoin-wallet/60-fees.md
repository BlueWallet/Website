---
title: Where can I learn about fees, RBF, and speeding up a transaction?
categories: [bitcoin-wallet]
---

On-chain wallets let you set fees and bump, cancel, or batch transactions with RBF and CPFP. If a payment sits unconfirmed, open it from the list ([transaction status](/docs/transaction-status/)) or check [mempool.space](https://mempool.space) with the transaction ID.
