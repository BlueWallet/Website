---
title: Can I import a wallet from another app?
categories: [bitcoin-wallet]
---

Yes. Use **Add wallet → Import wallet**, then paste or scan a recovery phrase, private key, public key, or wallet backup. BlueWallet detects common standards, including Electrum seeds and BRD/Breadwallet. Details: [Import a wallet](/docs/import-wallet/). If the balance is wrong, see passphrase and custom derivation in the [docs FAQ](/docs/#faq).
