---
title: Does it work with hardware wallets?
categories: [watch-only]
---

Yes. Import the hardware wallet as watch-only using its extended public key, build the transaction in BlueWallet, sign the PSBT on the device (QR or file), then bring the signed transaction back to broadcast. This works with PSBT-compatible hardware wallets. Guide: [Sign a transaction offline](/docs/sign-offline/) and [Use BlueWallet offline as a cold wallet](/docs/offline-cold-wallet/).
