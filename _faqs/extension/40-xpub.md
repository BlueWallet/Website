---
title: Should I add an address or an xpub?
categories: [extension]
---

Use an **extended public key** (xpub, ypub, or zpub) to watch a whole account as new coins arrive. Add a **single address** only when you want to track that one output. zpub is the usual pair for a Native SegWit wallet. You can copy an xpub from [Wallet details](/docs/wallet-details/) in the BlueWallet app.
