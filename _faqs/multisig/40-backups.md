---
title: What is the difference between the Vault backup and the watch-only backup?
categories: [multisig]
---

The **Vault backup** includes signing material and is a secret. The **watch-only (coordination) backup** is the map of public keys needed to reconstruct the setup if you lose one seed. Store both; the coordination backup can be copied more widely than the keys themselves. See [Multisig Vault General](/docs/multisig-wallet/).
