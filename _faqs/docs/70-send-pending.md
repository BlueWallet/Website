---
title: How do I send bitcoin, and why is a transaction still pending?
categories: [docs]
---

Open the wallet → **Send**, enter address and amount, review the fee, then confirm. Guide: [Send a Bitcoin transaction](/docs/send-bitcoin-transaction/). Unconfirmed sends sit in the mempool until miners include them; a higher fee confirms faster. Tap the payment for [transaction status](/docs/transaction-status/), **Speed up** / **Cancel** when available, or look the ID up on [mempool.space](https://mempool.space).
