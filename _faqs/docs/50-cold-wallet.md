---
title: Can I use BlueWallet as a cold wallet?
categories: [docs]
---

Yes. Keep a spare phone permanently offline (Airplane Mode + BlueWallet Offline Mode), hold the seed there, and use a watch-only wallet on your everyday phone to build transactions. Pair PSBT-compatible hardware wallets the same way. Guide: [Use BlueWallet offline as a cold wallet](/docs/offline-cold-wallet/). Also [Sign a transaction offline](/docs/sign-offline/).
