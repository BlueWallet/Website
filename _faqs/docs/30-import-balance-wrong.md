---
title: I imported my seed, but the balance is wrong. What should I try?
categories: [docs]
---

If the backup used a BIP39 passphrase (25th word), enable **Passphrase** in the import **⋯** menu: [Import with a passphrase](/docs/import-passphrase/). If discovery still misses the wallet, turn on [Search accounts](/docs/import-search-accounts/), then [Custom derivation path](/docs/import-custom-derivation/) if you know the path from the original app.
