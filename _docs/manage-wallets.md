The home screen shows your wallets as cards in a carousel. **Manage Wallets** is where you reorder them, hide balances, and jump quickly into a wallet.

## Open Manage Wallets

**Long-press** any wallet card on the home screen.

{% include phone-screenshot-image.html img="manage-wallets-01-home.png" alt="Home screen with wallet cards" %}

The **Manage Wallets** screen opens with a list of all your wallets.

{% include phone-screenshot-image.html img="manage-wallets-02-manage-screen.png" alt="Manage Wallets screen with drag to reorder hint" %}

## Reorder wallets

When you have more than one wallet, drag a row up or down to change the order on the home screen. The hint at the top reminds you that rows can be dragged.

## Hide a wallet balance

**Swipe left** on a wallet row to reveal **Hide**. Tap it to conceal the balance in this list (and on the home screen card).

{% include phone-screenshot-image.html img="manage-wallets-03-swipe-hide.png" alt="Swipe left on a wallet to reveal Hide" %}

The balance is replaced with a hidden placeholder until you show it again.

{% include phone-screenshot-image.html img="manage-wallets-04-balance-hidden.png" alt="Wallet row with hidden balance" %}

Swipe left again and tap **Show** to bring the balance back.

## Other actions

- **Tap a wallet** in the list to open it.
- Tap **✕** in the top-left corner to return to the home screen.

## Tips

- Hiding a balance is useful on a shared or demo device — it does not affect the wallet itself.
- Use [search](/docs/search-wallets/) on this screen to find a wallet by name, address, transaction, or memo.
