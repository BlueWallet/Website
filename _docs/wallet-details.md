**Wallet details** is the settings screen for a single wallet. Open it from the wallet screen by tapping **⋯** in the top-right corner. From here you can rename the wallet, review stats, change display options, and access backup or advanced information.

## Name and stats

At the top you see the wallet **Name**. Tap **edit** to rename it.

The two stat boxes show:

- **Transactions** — how many on-chain transactions this wallet has. The **⋯** menu on this box can export your transaction history to CSV.
- **Coins** — how many spendable UTXOs (coins) the wallet holds. Tap it to open [Coin control](/docs/coin-control/) when coins are available.

**Show addresses** lists the receive addresses generated by this wallet.

## Options

The **Options** section controls how the wallet behaves:

- **Display in Home Screen** — when on, this wallet’s transactions appear in the main feed below the wallet carousel. Turn it off to hide activity from the home screen while keeping the wallet.
- **Reusable and shareable code (BIP47)** — enables a [payment code](https://github.com/bitcoin/bips/blob/master/bip-0047.mediawiki) for private, reusable receiving addresses. Not all services support BIP47.
- **Show Wallet XPUB** — displays the extended public key. Useful for watch-only setups or importing into another app. Safe to share for viewing balances; never share your seed phrase.
- **Sign/Verify Message** — prove ownership of an address by signing a message, or check a signature from someone else. See [Sign and verify a message](/docs/sign-verify-message/).

Wallet types differ — Lightning, multisig, and watch-only wallets may show additional rows here.

## Advanced, backup, and delete

Scroll down and tap **Advanced** to expand technical details:

- **Type** — the wallet standard (for example, HD SegWit BIP84 Bech32 Native).
- **Master Fingerprint** — tap **View** to see the key fingerprint used by hardware wallets and PSBT workflows.
- **Derivation path** — the BIP32 path used to derive addresses (for example, `m/84'/0'/0'`).

{% include phone-screenshot-image.html img="wallet-details-02-options-advanced.png" alt="Wallet details advanced section, export, and delete" %}

Below **Advanced**:

- **Export/Backup** — save your recovery phrase or other export format. See [Backup and export a wallet](/docs/backup-wallet/) for a full walkthrough.
- **Delete Wallet** — removes the wallet from this device. You must confirm, and if the wallet still has a balance you will be asked to enter the amount as a safety check. Your backup is required to recover funds after deletion.

## Tips

- Renaming a wallet only changes the label in BlueWallet — it does not affect addresses or your backup.
- Turning off **Display in Home Screen** does not delete transactions; it only hides them from the home feed.
- Always back up before deleting a wallet or switching devices.
